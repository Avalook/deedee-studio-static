MAKING WAVES - VALORANT

Genesis Motion Technology | Riot Games

Games

DeeDee collaborated with Genesis Motion Technologies (Singapore) to produce the MV "Making Waves" for the VALORANT Champions Tour Pacific 2023.

The VALORANT Champions Tour Pacific is an annual professional esports tournament that brings together teams from the Asia-Pacific region to compete in the popular first-person shooter game, VALORANT. The tournament is hosted by Riot Games and is part of the larger VALORANT Champions Tour (VCT).
