Filmmaking techniques

Animation

The film is entirely hand-drawn, a deliberate choice to let our team of animators fully express their artistry especially in dynamic, expressive action scenes. The character designs are intentionally simple yet rooted in Vietnamese identity, allowing personality and culture to shine through movements and expressions.

Background

The backgrounds are rendered in a realistic style, deeply inspired by the textures of post-war Vietnam: the yellow-stained walls of old houses, narrow alleyways lined with memories, the distant calls of street vendors, the morning routines of elders in the park. These are not just backdrops- they are emotional landscapes.

Choosing a challenging artistic route, the film features gouache-inspired backgrounds that require a profound understanding of color and a wealth of traditional handcrafting techniques

The artists went on many field trips to capture the most realistic and relatable details for every single frame

The team

DeeDee Animation Studio

With a proven track record of delivering high-quality animation across acclaimed feature films and projects featuring globally recognized brands and characters of Marvel, DC Comics, and Disney, DeeDee Animation Studio has established itself as one of Vietnam’s leading 2D animation studios. Beyond fostering long-term partnerships with international clients and producing animated advertising campaigns for major domestic brands, DeeDee has also built a distinctive creative identity through original IP-driven projects. These works have earned prestigious accolades, including the Vietnam Film Festival and the Golden Kite Awards, while also receiving recognition and awards at international film festivals.

At this year’s Annecy International Animation Film Festival, DeeDee Animation Studio is proud not only to participate in the Vietnam Pavilion but also to see its animated short film Home Again selected for The Animated Thrills and Chills program. This achievement marks a significant milestone in the studio’s journey to bring Vietnamese animation closer to global audiences and further showcase the creative potential of Vietnam’s animation industry on the international stage.
