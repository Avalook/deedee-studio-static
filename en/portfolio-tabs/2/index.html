our work

At DeeDee Animation Studio, we believe exceptional animation begins long before the first frame is drawn. More than an animation production house, we provide the creative direction and production leadership that transform bold ideas into premium 2D animation.

​

Working with leading studios, production companies, and global brands, we specialize in young adult and adult-oriented animation across both long-form and short-form formats. Combining cinematic storytelling with production excellence, our expertise spans hand-drawn animation, rigging, and cut-out animation.

​

Every project is driven by one goal: delivering animated stories that resonate long after the final frame.
